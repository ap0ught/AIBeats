So is this a T A S? Yeah, it is. A tool assisted speedrun is built on an emulator using save states, and then played back as a file of button presses, and that's exactly what this is. The difference is how it got built. Most of them are made by people going frame by frame, sometimes for months. This one was built by an AI writing its own search tools, in twelve days, and it stuck to the human rules the whole time.
